### ⚙️ Thông Tin Quy Chuẩn & Điều Kiện Môn Học
- **Thang điểm:** 10
- **Điểm trung bình tối thiểu để qua môn (MinAvgMarkToPass):** `5`
- **Phân bổ thời gian học (Time Allocation):** Study hour (500h)= 33.75h contact hours (1newslot/week*15week) + 1.5h capstone project defense + 464.75h self-study
- **Phương pháp giảng dạy:** Inquiry-based Teaching,Experiential learning methods,Project Based Training
- **Quyết định ban hành:** 377/QĐ-ĐHFPT dated 04/09/2026
- **Yêu cầu chuyên cần & sinh viên (Student Tasks):**
> # Choose appropriate project and form group.# Divide and schedule work among members.# Attend all scheduled meeting with supervisor.# Produce document and product according to project requirement and member work share.# Defend work infront of examination board, showing both project results and individual responsibility.# In case of cheating found, the whole team is not allow to defend.#Students are required to attend at least 80% of scheduled meetings with their supervisor(s) to be eligible for the thesis or capstone defense.
- **Công cụ & phần mềm yêu cầu (Tools & Software):**
> Appropriate tools for design and implementation.
